VUONG QUOC TRO CHOI - BAT DAU TAI DAY

1) Giai nen toan bo NOI DUNG trong ZIP vao thu muc "vuong quoc tro choi" ma ban da tao.
2) Mo folder do trong VS Code. Mo terminal tai thu muc chua package.json.
3) PowerShell: npm.cmd install
4) PowerShell: npm.cmd run dev
5) Mo http://127.0.0.1:5173 trong Chrome/Edge.
6) Chon "Luyen tap webcam" -> "Bat webcam" -> cho phep su dung camera.
7) Neu ban dang dung Codex CLI, hay dan noi dung trong docs/CODEX_PROMPT.txt
   (hoac yeu cau Codex doc AGENTS.md va docs/CHAT_HANDOFF.md).
